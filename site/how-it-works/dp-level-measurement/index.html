How DP Level Measurement Works

Fill the tank and change the liquid density. The transmitter is calibrated for water, so watch the shown level drift when the specific gravity changes.

Live animationAdjustable slidersFormula and example

Quick answer: DP level measurement uses the pressure at the bottom of a tank. Liquid head pressure equals density × g × height, so a DP transmitter calibrated for a known density converts pressure into level.

DP Level Measurement Working Principle Step by Step

  1. The H side of the transmitter connects near the tank bottom; the L side sees the gas space or atmosphere.
  2. Liquid above the tap pushes down with pressure P = ρ × g × h.
  3. The transmitter is ranged for that pressure at the design density, for example 0 to 49.03 kPa for 0 to 5 m of water.
  4. Output is 4 to 20 mA in proportion to pressure and therefore to level.
  5. If the real density differs from the design density, the shown level is wrong in the same ratio.

DP Level Measurement Formula

P = ρ × g × h
h = P ÷ (ρ × g)
Span for 5 m water: 1000 × 9.80665 × 5 = 49.03 kPa
Shown level = actual level × (actual SG ÷ design SG)

Advantages

  • Simple, proven and inexpensive
  • Works with foam, vapour and most liquids
  • Easy to calibrate and verify

Limitations

  • Reading depends on density
  • Closed tanks need wet or dry leg corrections
  • Plugged taps or impulse lines cause errors

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does density affect DP level?

The transmitter measures pressure, not height. A lighter liquid gives less pressure for the same height, so it reads low unless the density is corrected.

What about closed tanks?

Connect the L side to the vapour space so tank pressure cancels out. A wet leg or remote seals may be needed, with zero suppression or elevation.

Where should the H tap be?

At or below the lowest level you need to measure, as the transmitter cannot see below the tap.

What is zero suppression?

It shifts the range when the transmitter is mounted below the tap, so the extra liquid head in the line is not read as level.
