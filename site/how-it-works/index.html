Frequently Asked Questions

What are these How It Works pages?

Animated diagrams of common process instruments. Move the sliders to change flow, level, pressure or temperature and watch the instrument respond, with the formula and the main parts explained.

Are the numbers real?

The formulas are standard engineering relations and the thermocouple page uses the IEC 60584 type K table. Some animations exaggerate small effects so they are easy to see; the pages say where.

Do the animations work on mobile?

Yes. They run in any modern browser, pause when scrolled out of view, and respect the reduced motion setting.
