How a Radar Level Transmitter Works

Change the level and the liquid's dielectric constant. Watch the microwave pulse travel down and back, and the echo peak move and change size.

Live animationAdjustable slidersFormula and example

Quick answer: A radar level transmitter sends microwave signals from an antenna at the top of the tank. They reflect from the liquid surface, and the time of flight gives the distance; level is tank height minus that distance.

Radar Level Transmitter Working Principle Step by Step

  1. The antenna directs microwaves down towards the liquid surface.
  2. Part of the energy reflects at the surface, more for liquids with a high dielectric constant.
  3. The transmitter measures the round trip time (pulse) or frequency shift (FMCW).
  4. It builds an echo curve and picks the surface echo, ignoring known false echoes.
  5. Distance = c × t ÷ 2, and level = reference height minus distance.

Radar Level Transmitter Formula

d = c × t ÷ 2
c ≈ 299,792,458 m/s in air or vapour
Level = H(ref) − d
Reflection ≈ ((√εr − 1) ÷ (√εr + 1))²
Example: 4 m distance gives t ≈ 26.7 ns

Main Parts of a Radar Level Transmitter

PartWhat it does
AntennaHorn, lens or drop antenna that focuses the beam
Process sealSeparates electronics from the tank
ElectronicsGenerates the signal, builds the echo curve and outputs level
Mounting nozzlePosition and length affect false echoes

Advantages

  • Non contact: no parts in the liquid
  • Not affected by density, temperature or pressure changes
  • Works on most liquids and many solids

Limitations

  • Low dielectric liquids give weak echoes
  • Foam, internals and nozzles can cause false echoes
  • Setup of the echo curve needs care

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dielectric constant and why does it matter?

It describes how a material reacts to an electric field. Water (about 80) reflects strongly; oils (about 2) reflect only a few percent, so the echo is weaker.

Does vapour affect radar?

In most cases very little, which is a big advantage over ultrasonic. Very high pressure vapours can slightly slow the signal.

What is a false echo?

A reflection from something other than the surface, such as an agitator, ladder or weld. False echo mapping teaches the transmitter to ignore it.

Pulse or FMCW?

Pulse radar measures time directly; FMCW sweeps frequency and measures the difference frequency. Both give the same distance.
