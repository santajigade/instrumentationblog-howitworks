Frequently Asked Questions

Are these instrumentation calculators free?

Yes. Every calculator is free to use, works on mobile and desktop, and needs no download or sign up.

How accurate are the calculators?

Each tool is built on the published standard for its topic, such as NIST ITS-90 for thermocouples and IEC 60751 for RTDs, and shows the formula so you can check every step.

How often are new calculators added?

New categories are released regularly. New tools appear on their category page and in the Newest Calculators list.
