Frequently Asked Questions

Are these instrumentation tools free?

Yes. Every tool is free, works on mobile and desktop, and needs no download or sign up.

What tools are available?

Instrument tag decoding, IP, NEMA and Ex marking decoders, wire colour codes, PID tuning with a simulator, Modbus and HART protocol tools, interactive troubleshooting guides, practice quizzes, P&ID symbols, pipe and flange charts and thread charts.

How is this different from the calculators?

Calculators solve engineering equations. Tools decode, look up and explain field information such as tags, markings, colour codes and tuning rules.
