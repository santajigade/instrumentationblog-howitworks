Commissioning Check for Blocking Distance

Datasheets measure blocking distance from the sensor reference point, and that point is not the same on every instrument. On many radar transmitters it is the underside of the flange or the thread face, while on some ultrasonic sensors it is the face of the transducer. Before you calculate the mounting height, confirm which reference the manufacturer uses and add the nozzle length, because a 200 mm nozzle adds directly to the distance between the reference point and the maximum level.

During commissioning, fill the vessel slowly to the maximum operating level while you watch the level reading and the echo strength. The level should rise smoothly and stay stable at the top band. If the value freezes, jumps to full scale or drops to empty as the product nears the nozzle, the surface is entering the near zone. Note the highest level where the reading stays reliable and set the high alarm and the overfill trip below it, with a margin for surface waves and foam.
