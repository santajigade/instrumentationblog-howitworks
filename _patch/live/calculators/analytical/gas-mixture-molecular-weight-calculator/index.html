Mixture Properties

MWmix = Σ(yi × MWi)
SG = MWmix ÷ 28.9647
ρ = P × MW ÷ (R × T)

yi is the mole fraction of each component (equal to volume fraction for ideal gases), MWi its molecular weight, 28.9647 the molecular weight of dry air, P the absolute pressure, T in kelvin and R = 8.314 J/mol·K. Density assumes ideal gas (Z = 1); at high pressure divide by Z.

Worked example

A natural gas of 90 % methane, 5 % ethane, 2 % propane, 1 % CO₂ and 2 % N₂ has MW ≈ 17.82 g/mol, SG ≈ 0.615 and a density of about 0.754 kg/m³ at 15 °C and 1.01325 bar a.

Key insight: a gas chromatograph result feeds straight into flow computers this way: MW and density from the composition correct orifice and ultrasonic gas flow measurements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is mole percent the same as volume percent?

For ideal gases, yes. Most gas analyses are reported in mol % or vol %.

Why is the result normalised?

Analyses rarely add to exactly 100 %. Normalising scales each component so the total is 100 %.

When is ideal gas density not accurate?

At high pressure or near condensation. Use a compressibility factor from AGA 8 or GERG for natural gas at pipeline pressure.
